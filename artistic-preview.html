A little about the music

Faith is where the songs begin.

Musicians of Christ is the artist name I use for my music. I started building this work independently in 2020, under the Jena Alvin Label.

Some songs come from a place of praise; others hold space for the harder seasons. I hope each one gives you a moment to breathe, reflect, and draw closer to God.

“I want the music to carry a message of faith, comfort, and hope.”
Meet the artist

Jena Alvin

Musicians of Christ is my stage name and artist name. It’s the name I release music under and the heart behind this ministry.

I write, sing, and create from my faith and the things I’ve lived through. My sound moves between soulful singing, rap, and worship, with one purpose at its center: to share the love of Christ.

I also compose and create my own beats from scratch, shaping the sound and feel of each song from the very beginning.

These pictures offer a glimpse of the artist behind the songs. Thanks for being here and taking the time to listen.

Licensing inquiries

Interested in using a Musicians of Christ song in a film, documentary, advertisement, or another project? Email the details below so I can understand what you have in mind and respond with the next steps.

What to include in your email

  • Project type: Commercial, independent film, documentary, social media promo, or another format.
  • Track title(s): Name the song or instrumental you’re interested in.
  • Use and territory: Explain where and how the music will be used, and the intended territory (for example, worldwide web or regional television).
  • Project budget: Share your estimated music-licensing budget.
  • Timing: Include any important production or release deadlines.
Send licensing inquiries to musiciansofchrist@yahoo.com.
