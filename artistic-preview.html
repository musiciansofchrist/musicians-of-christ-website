A little about the music

Faith is where the songs begin.

Musicians of Christ is the artist name I use for my music. I started building this work independently in 2020, under the Jena Alvin Label.

Some songs come from a place of praise; others hold space for the harder seasons. I hope each one gives you a moment to breathe, reflect, and draw closer to God.

“I want the music to carry a message of faith, comfort, and hope.”
Meet the artist

Jena Alvin

Musicians of Christ is my stage name and artist name. It’s the name I release music under and the heart behind this ministry.

I write, sing, and create from my faith and the things I’ve lived through. My sound moves between soulful singing, rap, and worship, with one purpose at its center: to share the love of Christ.

These pictures offer a glimpse of the artist behind the songs. Thanks for being here and taking the time to listen.
