About Musicians of Christ

Musicians of Christ is an independent Christian artist and band dedicated to spreading the Gospel through powerful, Spirit-led music.

Founded and led by visionary artist Jena Alvin, Musicians of Christ has been operating independently under the Jena Alvin Label since 2020. The ministry is anchored in worship, truth, and the transformative power of God’s Gospel.

Their expressive sound blends soulful singing, worship anthems, and a powerful presence of musicianship that creates an immersive experience. Every song is written to touch hearts, renew spirits, and usher listeners into the presence of God.

Through live performances, original recordings, and ministry-driven collaborations, the group creates music that speaks to the brokenhearted, uplifts the weary, and points people toward Jesus Christ.

Meet the Founder

A creative leader devoted to using music as a vessel for worship, deliverance, and spiritual breakthrough.

Jena Alvin
Founder, Leader & Creative Force

Jena Alvin is the visionary founder, leader, and driving creative force behind Musicians of Christ. Operating under the Jena Alvin Label since 2020, Jena is a multi-talented independent artist, performer, songwriter, composer, and keyboardist.

Her dynamic ministry breaks traditional boundaries by blending soulful singing with powerful, faith-filled rapping and worship songs, delivering the Gospel message with modern energy and deep conviction. Backed by a dedicated group of skilled musicians, Jena pours her heart into arranging and crafting music that completely honors God.

Her unwavering commitment to faith and creative excellence fuels her divine calling to uplift heavy hearts and share Christ’s transformative love through song.
